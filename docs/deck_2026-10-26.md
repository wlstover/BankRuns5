## 1. Validation against Ch 1 (rung 1, pre-registered)

**1a. Sorting follows the public signal's error.** `e01_sorting.png`
- x: the public signal's gap at tick 1, y₁ − θ (left = pessimistic, right = optimistic).
  y: change in D against the same run with no public signal. One line per lambda pair,
  CI ribbons; shaded regions are where Ch 1 predicts a sign (G4′ left, G1′ right).
- Lines rise left to right through zero: optimistic y → individualists run more
  relative to collectivists; pessimistic → collectivists run more. Effect scales with the
  lambda gap: (0.1, 0.9) reaches about ±0.1, (0.3, 0.5) stays flat near zero.

**1b. Who withdraws, across fundamentals.** `e02_whoruns.png`
- Three columns: no public signal, stale y₀ = 0.2, stale y₀ = 0.8 (lambda pair 0.1/0.9).
  Top strip: P(fail) by θ bin (grey bars). Bottom: share who withdraw by type, orange vs
  blue, with CIs. Shaded "coordination band" (θ 0.1–0.4); dashed line at θ = y₀.
- No signal: collectivists withdraw more at low θ, both fall to ~0 above θ 0.5.
  Pessimistic stale y keeps both types running across all θ (P(fail) ~0.4 even at high
  θ). Optimistic stale y shuts runs down almost everywhere except θ < 0.1.

**1c. The tests hold across three versions of the bank.** `e03_tests_by_bank.png`
- Five panels (G1′, G4′, G2a′, G3, G3c); rows = risk-neutral all-or-nothing (r1t), CARA
  partial (r1c), capped reference (r1k). Filled dot + CI = pooled; hollow markers = each
  lambda pair. Shaded side = Ch 1's predicted sign.
- Numbers (r1k, pooled): G1′ +0.033, G4′ −0.031, G2a′ +0.093, all Holm-significant;
  G2b ordering holds. G3 +0.0034 pooled but the (0.3, 0.5) pair sits on the wrong side
  in every bank → **pre-registered verdict: G3 counts against**. G3c (post hoc,
  channel-isolated) +0.019, right-signed in every pair.
- Verdict line: 3 of 4 confirmatory tests support, in all three banks.

## 3. Cultural composition (μ, λ gap)

**3a. More individualists, fewer failures.** `e05_pfail_by_mu.png`
- x: individualist share μ (0, .25, .5, .75, 1). y: P(fail). One line per lambda pair.
- (0.1, 0.9): 0.67 at μ 0 → 0.08 at μ 1; (0.2, 0.8) similar; (0.3, 0.5): 0.25 → 0.09.
  Monotone, no interior peak. The lambda gap matters most when collectivists dominate;
  the lines converge at μ ≥ 0.75.
- ⚠️ Ch 1 sets up non-monotonic fragility (P6). It does not appear on this bank; the
  chapter's earlier non-monotonic result was on the vault bank. Decide the framing.

**3b. Collectivists withdraw more at every mix.** `e06_withdraw_by_mu.png`
- Three panels (lambda pairs). x: μ. y: share who withdraw, orange vs blue, CIs. Each
  line ends where its type is absent (no I at μ 0, no C at μ 1).
- Both fall with μ; collectivists above individualists at every mixed μ for the two wide
  gaps; nearly identical at (0.3, 0.5).

**3c. The mechanism: collectivists herd.** `e07_herding.png`
- x: share of the agent's neighbours who have withdrawn. y: P(withdraw | deciding).
  Blue vs orange, direct-labelled.
- Collectivists jump from 0.15 to 0.93 once 20–35% of neighbours are out and reach 1.0;
  individualists plateau at ~0.25–0.30. Slopes: C 0.80, I 0.22 (S3b C − I +0.58, t 16.7).

## 4. Information structure

**4a. A stale public signal moves failure, in both directions.** `e08_pfail_by_signal.png`
- Rows: public-signal arm (stale y₀ 0.2, none, tracks θ, stale y₀ 0.5, stale y₀ 0.8),
  sorted by failure. x: P(fail), dot + CI per lambda pair. All μ.
- (0.1, 0.9): stale pessimistic 0.48, none 0.31, tracks 0.29, stale 0.5 0.18, stale 0.8
  0.16. An accurate public signal barely changes failure; a stale optimistic one halves
  it; a stale pessimistic one raises it by half. (0.3, 0.5) barely moves (0.09–0.15).

**4b. Which channel carries culture.** `e09_channels.png`
- Three groups: λ applied only to the head-count blend (neighbours vs private), only to
  the θ-signal blend (public vs private), or both (the model). y: D in failed runs where y
  was optimistic, dot + CI per lambda pair.
- θ-blend only: +0.14 (Ch 1's sign). Head-count only: −0.07 (opposite). Both: +0.02.
  The two channels pull in opposite directions; Ch 1's sign survives in the combined
  model, damped by herding. This is why G3 needs the base subtracted (G3c).

## 5. Payoffs and contracts

**5a. Deposit concentration as a design feature.** `e10_concentration.png`
- x: μ. y: P(fail), lambda pairs pooled. Three greys: sigma 1 (no concentration), sigma
  2.5 capped (reference), sigma 2.5 uncapped.
- Uncapped 0.61 → 0.11; capped 0.51 → 0.08; sigma 1 0.50 → 0.08. Capping the largest
  deposits brings failure down to the no-concentration bank's level at every μ. The μ
  gradient is the same in all three: culture's effect doesn't depend on concentration.
- Also from the pilots (no figure yet): the fire-sale discount d is the lever for where
  failures sit (F10); CARA makes large depositors split, and raises P(fail) (F12b).
- ⚠️ Insurance and R have not been run on the fire-sale bank. Vault-bank results exist
  (chapter Experiments 1–2); label them "vault bank, earlier specification" if used.
